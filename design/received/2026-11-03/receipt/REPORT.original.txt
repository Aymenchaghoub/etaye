## Findings fixed during this session (highlights)

- **Ambiguous medical wording:** AI processing was labelled *Traitement(s)*, so the home page said "Aucun traitement en cours" next to patients' treatments. It is now *Tâche(s)* everywhere: navigation, status axis, banners, documents.
- **Dialogs:** long dialogs scrolled their actions out of view. Their body now scrolls, and the title and actions stay visible (shared CSS).
- **Stacked tables:** action buttons overflowed at 320 px. E-mail addresses broke mid-word (they now break only after "@" and before dots).
- **Generated content:** pre-generation snapshots showed source markers and "Citée". The wrong decision was preselected on the unsupported passage. A patient explanation was labelled "jamais publié".
- **PDFs:** footer margin boxes wrapped. The micro sign (µ) fell back to DejaVu because Atkinson Hyperlegible has no U+00B5. A source list could be orphaned across a page break.
- **E-mails:** the plain-text version leaked HTML markup. Mockup annotations sat inside the e-mail copy.
- **Consultation and publication batch:** a helper session built it and stopped at a rate limit before reporting. Claude rebuilt, re-rendered and re-opened all of its output, and no helper claim is used as evidence.

## Decisions for Aymen

1. **PD-001**: whether schedulers may register patients, which is shown as a proposal in the "planificateur" variants.
2. **Login entry**: one login page with a patient-branded variant, or a separate patient entry URL. Also the password length rule (12 characters is a proposal).
3. **Visit preparation**: whether an explicit sharing consent is needed to send it (proposed), and whether patients may cancel online or only move an appointment.
4. **Consultation completion**: the guard where a missing reason blocks completion (proposed), and whether "Marquer comme lue" is needed on S-17.
5. **Withdrawal label**: "Retirer l'accès" (spec I-18) or "Retirer la publication" (AGENTS label list).
6. **Invitation validity**: 72 hours is a proposal. Also whether an administrator may grant clinical capabilities, which the access matrix allows with TEAM_MANAGE.
7. **µ in the interface**: keep the per-glyph fallback, or add Source Serif 4 to `--font-donnee`.
8. **Spec copy deviation**: I-23's title "Le traitement a échoué" now reads "La tâche a échoué".
