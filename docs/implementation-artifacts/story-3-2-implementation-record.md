## 1. What the story delivers

A read-only exploration of a patient record, on the real stack (Spring, PostgreSQL with row-level security, React):

- **Contract first** (three commits before any application code): `getHistorySummary` (the derived state of the three history sections), the `kind` filter of `getTimeline`, the `category` filter of `listAssertions`, `asOf` on `getSourceRevision`, optional provenance fields on `TimelineItem` (reference, source type, author, revision, correction reason, excerpt) and `Source` (reference, kind, label, excerpt, correction reason, superseded reference), the optional `sourceKind` and recording date of `Assertion`, and the planning-contract correction of `Assertion` (G-17): `text`, `sourceType`, `correctionReason` and `sourceRevisionId` become optional on output because an UNKNOWN statement has none of them, `recordedAt` becomes required. The README section "Record evidence reads", the BR-008 section-state table and the data dictionary rows were written with it. The generated Web SDK was regenerated and the drift check passes. The contract has 100 operations.
- **Data.** `V9__record_evidence.sql`: `source_revision` and `clinical_assertion` with composite practice and record keys, a supersession foreign key that stays inside the record and lineage, checks that forbid a fabricated event date and require the correction reason, forced row-level security, `SELECT` only for the runtime role and triggers that reject any update or delete (a revision is superseded, never edited) and that keep the revision and recording order, and a zone check. `V10__seed_record_evidence.sql` (demo package): the synthetic fixture as one assertion per statement (HIST-A-001 becomes its two cited statements), the weight pair with the text the practitioner could read at the time (F-4), the UNKNOWN rows recorded at the dataset baseline (F-5), the V5 reset function replaced with the two tables added to its explicit `TRUNCATE` list (PostgreSQL refuses to truncate a referenced table otherwise) and a new `restore_fixture` wrapper that seeds the evidence after a reload.
- **API** (`records` module): `RecordEvidenceController`, `RecordEvidenceService`, `EvidenceRepository` (JDBC on the practice transaction), `SectionStateRule`. Every read requires the actor's current `CLINICAL_READ` membership in the route practice, resolves the record inside that practice, answers `Cache-Control: no-store` without an `ETag`, binds its cursor to practice, actor, record, view instant and filters, and refuses a future `asOf` with 400. A foreign, absent or guessed record or source is the same 404 as an absent one; an administration-only account is 403 with the bounded security event; routine reads are not audited (D-62).
- **Web** (`apps/web/src/record`): S-09 (chronology, filters, in-place sources, sections panel), S-10 (the three sections, dated statements, the read subset of I-27), the identity banner with the derived allergy state, the past-instant control and banner (DF-17), loading, partial and full failure, restricted (S-36 layout) and not-found states. The patient list name and the S-08 duplicate panel open S-09 (they led to the identity form in 3.1).
- **Evidence and CI**: isolation matrix extended with eight source cases and the `hidden-source` parity group (D-55), role catalog verification extended, reset test extended (D-54), four new API suites and two Web spec files required by the workflows, a real-stack spec.

Not delivered, by decision (UD-67): entering or correcting assertions, I-05 on S-10, the I-27 human disposition (“Conserver à vérifier”) and its toast, the capability projection (D-60, increment 2); other timeline contributions (D-61); a read audit (D-62); identity history in the as-of view (D-63). The demo journey of 9.1 is unchanged; the demo README describes how to read a record.

## 4. Differences from the approved specimens (disclosed, none approved)

1. **Weight correction line.** The list says “version précédente conservée · motif : {reason as written}”; the specimen paraphrases “ancienne valeur 86,4 kg conservée · motif : erreur de saisie”, which no API field supports.
2. **Origin chip.** The “Déclaré par la patiente · non confirmé” chip appears only on a patient preparation; a patient-reported consultation note (SRC-A-001) shows its origin in words and not the “Absence déclarée” chip of the specimen, because the timeline item does not carry its assertion. Its statement state is on S-10.
3. **I-27.** The specimen's “Champ manquant : nature et date de la réaction” is omitted: no field says what is missing. The note names the order of the two statements by their recording dates.
4. **Meta line of an empty record** reads “dates de survenue et d'enregistrement affichées séparément” where the specimen says “aucune source enregistrée”; the frame does not know the timeline is empty.
5. **Past-instant toggle** is present on both tabs and appears once the record is loaded; the specimen hides it until the whole page is loaded.
6. **Restricted state.** The S-36 layout is reused; the sentence reads “Votre accès actuel au {cabinet} ne donne pas accès aux dossiers patients”, and the navigation of the practitioner shell (one “Patients” entry) is shown to an administration-only account, as in 3.1.
7. **Time zone.** The practice time zone is not part of the session: the screens read and show Paris time (`Europe/Paris`), the dataset zone. The `practice.timezone` column exists and is not exposed.
8. **Header allergy line for a present state** lists each statement with its origin and date and wraps; it is never shortened.

## 5. Assumptions, findings and limits

- **F-4 and G-22 (text of SRC-A-007-v1).** The seed stores “Poids saisi : 86,4 kg.” so an as-of view between the two revisions never reveals the correction; the design fixture is unchanged. **F-5/G-23.** The three UNKNOWN rows carry no recording date in the fixture; they are recorded at the dataset baseline (2026-11-02 16:00), so a view before it reads “non renseigné”.
- **Revision order.** A later revision has the next revision number and a recording time not before its predecessor. V9 now enforces it with insert triggers (a correction follows its predecessor in revision number and recording time, an assertion is never recorded before the source it cites, a zone must be one PostgreSQL knows); the future append operations (D-60) inherit these guards.
- **[ASSUMPTION] Cursors.** A cursor holds the identifier of the last item and a binding; its position is read from the current view, so a cursor whose item left the view (a later correction of the current record) is a 400 and the client restarts the list. Immutable revisions and the as-of view cannot produce this today.
- **Pagination of sections.** A section lists at most 100 statements and says when it is truncated; its state is always derived from all of them.
- **Not run.** The real database-outage spec; HawkScan (no key, UD-47); Firefox and WebKit; screen readers; 200 % zoom; rendered-pixel contrast beyond axe-core; the demo journey of 9.1 (unchanged); a hosted CI run (the checks run on the pull request). The partial and full failures, the loading states and the long content could not be induced on the real stack without fault injection: those captures and the smoke cases use the stubbed API. G-18 (M2 budget) and G-20 (preparation statements as assertions) stay To verify.
