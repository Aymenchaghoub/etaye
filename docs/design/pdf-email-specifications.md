## Shared A4 composition and acceptance

Portrait A4, 210×297mm. [ASSUMPTION] Start with 18mm margins, 12mm header/footer safety region and a 10.5–11pt readable body; refine in Figma after long-content review. Existing source serif/Atkinson families and monochrome logo preserve identity. Never scale a UI screenshot to paper. Header: original logo, practice identity, document title, patient identity sufficient for this synthetic context. Metadata: audience, exact business status/publication, version, author/validator, generation/encounter/validation/publication dates separately when relevant. Footer: “Démonstration — personnes et données fictives”, document/version ID and “Page x / y”. No claim of certification or clinical readiness.

Selectable text, Unicode accents, nonbreaking value/unit and source references. Embed officially licensed fonts in future generation; verify missing glyphs, copy/paste and reading order. Aim for tagged semantic headings/tables/links and document language; test accessibility rather than claim PDF/UA conformance. Grayscale must preserve all meaning through labels, line treatment and symbols. No color-only abnormal/unknown distinction.

Long tables repeat headers and units, avoid splitting a row unless unavoidable, then repeat its identifier with “suite”. Keep heading with its first paragraph; prevent orphan source references; page-break at meaningful sections. Repeated patient identifier/document version makes detached pages understandable. Show source list with author/type/date/revision/locator, cross-linked statement numbers, and an “unavailable/unknown date” label when appropriate. Clinician references may include internal sources; patient exports only curated authorized citations/excerpts. No raw private object URL or hidden clinical annotation in PDF metadata.

## Email system

[ASSUMPTION] 600px maximum HTML reading width, original light/monochrome logo, restrained accent, readable 16px body, one clear safe-link action, plain-text equivalent. No image-only text. Subject/preheader/body/expiry/help/footer are designed together. Narrow 320px view and dark email clients must remain readable. Tokens and styling adapt conservatively because email clients differ. No clinical diagnosis, summary body, lab values or unnecessary patient identity in subject/notification text. Tokens appear only in expiring action links, never logs or screenshots containing real credentials. Example.test recipients go only to a local preview/sink.

All email links use configured allowlisted origin; never user-provided redirect URLs. Notification delivery state is separate from invitation/publication state. A failed email cannot unpublish a committed version or grant access itself. Audit includes event/template/version/outcome, not raw body or link token.
