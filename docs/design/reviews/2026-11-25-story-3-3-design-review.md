## Findings disclosed with the specimens

- **F-1 · Numeric cells never wrap in the received CSS.** `.etaye-tableau .etaye-col-num` is `white-space: nowrap`; a value of 12 integer and 6 decimal digits (the longest the contract accepts) overflowed its cell at 390 and 320 px (measured `scrollWidth` 179 against 173 and 142). AT-011-C forbids a clipped value. The specimens let numeric cells wrap below 760 px; the implementation must do the same. The first run of the checks found it before any capture was opened.
- **F-2 · Three tabs do not fit at 320 px.** The received tab bar scrolls horizontally (`overflow-x: auto`); with three tabs the current one ("Mesures") left the visible area at 320 px (found on an opened capture, not by the overflow assertion, which skips scroll containers). The specimens wrap the tabs below 420 px; the implementation of the shared record frame must do the same for the three tabs, which also affects S-09 and S-10 once the third tab exists.
- **F-3 · Gap rendering (card F1).** The design-system recipe says "missing periods drawn as gaps" and the reference draws a line with a disclaimer; the specimens follow the reference and the packet records the alternative (markers only). Aymen confirmed the segment as recommended (UD-72).
- **F-4 · Reason wording.** The reference summarises the correction as "erreur de saisie"; the specimens show the reason as stored ("Correction d’une erreur de saisie, 86,4 devient 68,4 kg."), the only text the seed and the API can supply. The design fixture writes the same reason with a colon where the seeded source revision writes a comma; the seed uses one wording (packet G-03 f).
- **F-5 · Relative periods (G-19).** "3 derniers mois" and "12 derniers mois" count back from the view instant, in the practice time zone (D-64 decided under UD-71 D1). Confirmed by Aymen as recommended (UD-72), no longer an assumption.
- **F-6 · Templates, not fixture records.** Mixed units, the incomplete blood-pressure group, long content and the 25-point series are fictional layout templates: "Mesure A", "unité A" and "unité B" carry no clinical meaning and the incomplete reading is flagged as a template on the page. The filter without result uses a real case (the view of 15/09/2026 holds the blood pressure of 14/09 but not yet the weight).
- **F-7 · The as-of view shows a wrong value on purpose.** At 10:14 the weight is 86,4 kg, the value entered before its correction, with no trace of the correction: that is the UD-32 contract. The practitioner reading the past instant sees what could be read then.
- **F-8 · Visible price of the read-only scope.** A wrong measurement is visible and its lineage readable, but it cannot be corrected from the screen until increment 2 (the S-10 situation of 3.2, finding F-9 there).

## Decision received

Aymen's written decision, separate from any development authorization, had two parts. (1) The 13 specimens: he chose “J’approuve ces états tels que présentés.”, so no modification and no change to any specimen. (2) Two points, both confirmed as recommended. The relative periods (G-19, F-5): “3 derniers mois” and “12 derniers mois” count back from the view instant (now, or the chosen past instant), in the practice time zone. The connecting segment (card F1): consecutive points are joined by a segment that carries no value, with the sentence “Le trait n’est pas une mesure”. The decision is recorded as UD-72, with `coverage.csv` and `html-reception.csv` rows for S-11 (I-06 unchanged).
