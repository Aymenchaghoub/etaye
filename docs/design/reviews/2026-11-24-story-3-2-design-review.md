## Findings disclosed with the specimens

- **Clipped dates in the received CSS.** Below 760 px the chronology gives its date column 64 px while “02/11/2026” needs 80 px, so the date is clipped (measured `scrollWidth` 80 against `clientWidth` 64 at 390 and 320 px). The approved S-09 reference has the same defect, and AT-010-C forbids a clipped date. The first generated specimens showed it; they now stack the date above the event below 760 px. The implementation must do the same.
- **Wording.** By the C1 table, “Traitements déclarés” of Claire and “Antécédents” of Louis read “Inconnu · à préciser” (their fixture rows are UNKNOWN statements); “Non renseigné” is reserved for a section with no statement. The packet's C2 and C3 wording is corrected accordingly.
- **Heading focus.** A route change focuses the heading without a ring (1.1, UD-64); controls keep their 2 px ring.
- **Fixture text that would leak.** The stored text of SRC-A-007-v1 ends with “Valeur historique corrigée ensuite”, which would reveal the later correction in the as-of view. The specimen shows the contemporaneous text; the seed must store it (packet gap G-22).
- **[ASSUMPTION]** The fixture's UNKNOWN rows carry no recording date; `s10-asof` assumes the seed records them at the dataset baseline (2026-11-02 16:00) (G-23, To verify).
- **Templates.** `s09-date-inconnue` and `s09-filtre-date` (an event without occurrence date), `s09-long` and `s10-long` (a statement of about 1,150 characters with a 70-character unbroken word and a label of more than 300 characters) are fictional layout templates; no dataset record has these cases.
- **Visible price of A1.** A section marked “à préciser” has no action to specify it until D-60. The review page names this in its fifth point.
