# Story 1.3 design feedback — I-20 close button

**User decision (UD-51, 2026-11-21).** Aymen accepted the references shown with a reservation on the blue outline around the close button. His words were “jete valide tout” and “si c le cas de hover ok c valide mais si c fait automattiquement c pas ok”. The attached image shows the received I-20 dialog at 1024×768. The decision covers the references presented in that exchange. Additional S-05 states, the I-20 practice-switch variant and development authorization remain pending. [Decision register](../../planning-artifacts/decisions-assumptions.md)

**Verified observation.** The frozen prototype calls `focus()` on the first candidate control. On the I-20 frame this is the close button. On a fresh 1024×768 Chromium page it was active, matched `:focus-visible` and had a solid outline while the pointer was elsewhere. [Frozen I-20 frame](../../../design/offline-reference/site/ecrans/I-20/ouvert.html) · [Prototype focus setup](../../../design/offline-reference/site/js/prototype.js#L67)

**Approved correction.** Aymen confirmed “je valide” after receiving the opening and keyboard-focus captures. Place initial programmatic focus on the dialog title to announce the decision. The title stays outside the Tab sequence and uses its normal heading appearance. The close button receives the standard outline when reached with Tab. The correction uses a temporary copy of the frozen mockup and keeps the origin frame and token values intact.

**Verified in Chromium 153.0.8010.12.** At 1440×1024, 1024×768, 390×844 and 320×844 in both themes, the title receives initial focus and the close button has no outline on opening or with simple pointer hover. Tab from the title focuses × with a visible outline. Shift+Tab from the title reaches the last action inside the dialog and Tab from there wraps to ×. The two tablet captures above and the 390 px opening view were inspected. Screen reader announcement, practice-switch variants and application behavior require later review.

**Reproduction boundary.** Starting from snapshot `d2f0800e275c…`, set `tabindex="-1"` on the I-20 heading, focus it at opening and suppress its non-interactive focus outline only. Allow Shift+Tab from the heading to wrap to the last action using the existing dialog focus loop. Leave the close button and its standard focus-visible rule in place. The [interaction catalog](../interaction-catalog.md#i-20) carries this requirement into the eventual build.
